COMMUNITY STANDARDS

SERVER RULES

Basic rules for now — expand these with your full RVRP rulebook later.

01

Respect Everyone

No harassment, hate speech, targeted toxicity, or disruptive behavior.

02

Value Your Life

Respond realistically to threats and dangerous situations. Your character's life matters.

03

No RDM / VDM

Do not randomly attack players or use vehicles as weapons without valid roleplay.

04

No Metagaming

Do not use information your character learned outside of roleplay.

05

No Powergaming

Do not force unrealistic actions or outcomes without allowing others a fair response.

06

Stay In Character

Keep active scenes immersive and avoid unnecessary OOC disruption.

07

New Life Rule

After a character-ending situation, do not immediately return for revenge or reuse forgotten scene information.

08

Follow Staff Direction

Follow staff instructions during active situations. Appeals and disputes can be handled afterward.

09

Common Sense

Not every situation needs a written rule. Use good judgment and protect the quality of RP.
