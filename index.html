COMMUNITY

SERVER RULES

01

Respect Everyone

Keep interactions respectful. Harassment, hate speech, and targeted toxicity are not welcome.

02

Value Your Life

Act as though your character's life matters and respond realistically to dangerous situations.

03

No RDM / VDM

Do not attack players without valid roleplay initiation or use vehicles as weapons without RP justification.

04

No Metagaming

Information learned outside your character's experience cannot be used in character.

05

No Powergaming

Do not force unrealistic actions or outcomes on another player without allowing meaningful response.

06

Follow Staff Direction

Staff decisions during active situations should be followed; disputes can be handled afterward.
